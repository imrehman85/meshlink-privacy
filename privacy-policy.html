MeshLink Privacy Policy

App: MeshLink (com.clevora.meshlink) · Developer: Clevora Technologies · Last updated: 11 November 2026

MeshLink is built to work without servers. Your data stays on your phone and on the phones you choose to talk to. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What we collect

Nothing. MeshLink has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. We (the developer) do not run any server that receives your data, and we never see your messages, contacts, location or usage.

What other nearby MeshLink users can see

  • Your display name and device ID (a fingerprint of your public key) are visible to MeshLink users on the mesh, so they can find and message you.
  • One-to-one messages, photos and videos are end-to-end encrypted. Only you and the person you're talking to can read them; phones that relay them cannot.
  • The "Everyone nearby" room is not encrypted: anyone nearby running MeshLink can read messages posted there. The app shows this clearly in that room.

Messages are stored only on the sending and receiving phones. Relaying phones may hold an encrypted copy for up to 24 hours to deliver it, then delete it.

Internet sharing and VPN

When you use internet from a nearby phone, MeshLink starts a local VPN on your device using Android's VpnService. The VPN only sends your traffic to the sharing phone you chose by scanning its QR code. MeshLink does not inspect, log, sell, modify or redirect that traffic, and it is not sent to us. As with any hotspot, the sharing phone's network can see which websites you connect to; use HTTPS websites for sensitive information. The phone that shares internet sees how much data each connected device uses, to apply data limits.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth, Nearby devices, Wi-Fi: to find and connect to nearby phones.
  • Location (Android 12 and older, or when a phone's Nearby service requires it): Android requires it for Bluetooth/Wi-Fi scanning. MeshLink never reads or stores your location.
  • Camera (optional): only to scan the QR code for internet sharing. No photos or videos are taken or saved.
  • Notifications: to show new messages and that the mesh or sharing is running.
  • Photos and videos: only the ones you pick with the system picker, to send them. Photos are re-encoded, which removes location and camera details.

Data retention and deletion

Your messages, media and identity keys are kept on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling MeshLink, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes everything. Nothing is kept anywhere else. App data is excluded from cloud backups and device transfers.

Children

MeshLink is not directed at children under 13.
